# Data card

Survey: LLM and Agent Security in Energy Systems: A Critical Survey. Release 1.1.0, prepared 2026-10-20; supporting records available in the linked GitHub repository.

The package contains 23 selected comparison records, 23 reporting appraisals, 17 numerical
claim locators, eight related-review scope checks, eight proposed metric definitions, a
472-record archive index and a 143-record exploratory census. The census sources are 104
archive matches, one targeted source, 23 journal-search additions and 15 citation-chasing additions.

Historical primary-purpose counts: T 8, C 7, D 17, V 7, E 46, R 29, G 21, B 3, X 5 (total 143).
These are not mutually exclusive scientific eligibility judgments: a V benchmark can also test
security. The former term 'direct evidence' for T+C+D was too broad; the paper now calls these
candidate security-focused streams and supplies classification sensitivity in S5.

All coding was AI-assisted. The source pass accessed 118 full texts and 25 abstracts. No human
has independently confirmed census codes or comparative annotations. Majority voting can retain
an abstract-based error despite contradictory full-text evidence. AI consistency statistics,
borderline records and alternative classifications are documented in S5. Historical codes are
preserved to keep this limitation inspectable.

Searches ran on 13 and 15 October 2026; the added Cheng et al. review was checked at institutional
abstract level on 20 October and is outside the frozen census. The collection is incomplete and
uses search ranking caps, English heuristics and security-targeted supplemental queries. Counts
must not be treated as field prevalence estimates or exhaustive counts of adversarial studies.

Machine-readable records identify the source edition and check basis. Numerical anchors locate
reported quantities; neither anchors nor AI interpretation replicate experiments. Source PDFs
are not redistributed. Repository: https://github.com/habibullahmanzoor/LLM-and-Agent-Security-in-Energy-Systems. The owner-selected MIT license is in the repository root; original publications retain their rights.
